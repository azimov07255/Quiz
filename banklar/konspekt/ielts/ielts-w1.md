# Writing Task 1: savol turlari bo‘yicha strategiya

## Task 1: tuzilma va Overview (Academic)
Grafik, jadval yoki diagrammadagi ma’lumotni 150+ so‘zda tasvirlash (20 daqiqa).

### Qadamlar
1. Kirish: mavzuni o‘z so‘zingiz bilan qayta ayting (paraphrase).
2. Overview: 2-3 ta eng muhim tendentsiya yoki xususiyat (sonlarsiz!). Bu bo‘lim bo‘lmasa, ball keskin tushadi.
3. 2 ta asosiy paragraf: tegishli ma’lumotlarni guruhlab, solishtirib tasvirlang.
4. Hamma sonni yozmang: muhim va tipik ma’lumotlarni tanlang. Birliklarni (%, million) yozing.

### Odatiy xatolar
- Overview’ning yo‘qligi.
- Fikr yoki sabab qo‘shish: faqat ma’lumotni tasvirlang.
- Hamma sonni ketma-ket sanab chiqish.

> **Vaqt:** 20 daqiqa: 3 daqiqa reja, 15 yozish, 2 tekshirish. **Band 8+ uchun:** Overview’ni kirishdan keyin darhol yozing: vaqt tugab qolsa ham eng muhim qism yozilgan bo‘ladi.

## Task 1: tendentsiya va taqqoslash tili
Ma’lumotni aniq va xilma-xil so‘zlar bilan ifodalash.

### Qadamlar
1. O‘zgarish: rise, increase, climb; fall, decline, plummet; fluctuate; remain stable; peak at; level off.
2. Darajasi: sharply, dramatically, steadily, gradually, slightly.
3. Taqqoslash: twice as high as, considerably lower than, the highest proportion, by contrast.
4. Taxminiy son: approximately, just under, roughly, nearly. Nisbat: a quarter, one in five.
5. Zamon ma’lumot davriga mos: o‘tgan yillar - past; kelajak yillar - will/is projected to.

### Odatiy xatolar
- Bir xil so‘zni takrorlash (rise, rise, rise).
- Zamonni adashtirish.

> **Band 8+ uchun:** Ma’noni chuqurroq beradigan tuzilmalardan foydalaning: “There was a sharp rise in X, which then levelled off at Y”.

## Task 1: jarayon va xarita
Process diagrammasi yoki xarita o‘zgarishini tasvirlash.

### Qadamlar
1. Jarayon: bosqichlarni ketma-ket so‘zlar bilan bog‘lang: First, Next, Subsequently, Finally. Ko‘pincha passiv zamon: is heated, is then filtered.
2. Overview: bosqichlar soni va asosiy g‘oya (boshidan oxirigacha nima bo‘ladi).
3. Xarita: avval-keyin farqini tasvirlang: was replaced by, was demolished, was converted into, a new road was built.
4. Yo‘nalishlar: to the north of, adjacent to, in the north-east.

### Odatiy xatolar
- Passiv zamon o‘rniga “people do” yozish.
- Xaritada hamma narsani sanash, asosiy o‘zgarishlarni ajratmaslik.

> **Vaqt:** 20 daqiqa. **Band 8+ uchun:** Jarayon tilida turli tuzilmalardan foydalaning: “Once the water has been filtered, it is stored…”.

## General Training: xat yozish (Task 1)
Vaziyat bo‘yicha rasmiy, yarim rasmiy yoki norasmiy xat (150+ so‘z).

### Qadamlar
1. Vaziyatni va kimga yozayotganingizni aniqlang: rasmiy (Dear Sir or Madam / Yours faithfully), tanish bo‘lmagan odam (Dear Mr Smith / Yours sincerely), do‘st (Hi Sam).
2. Topshiriqdagi 3 ta bandning HAMMASINI yoriting.
3. Tonni saqlang: rasmiy xatda qisqartmalar (I’m) va norasmiy so‘zlarsiz.
4. Qisqa kirish (maqsad), asosiy qism, yakun (qanday harakat kutilayotgani).

### Odatiy xatolar
- Bandlardan birini o‘tkazib yuborish.
- Ton aralashib ketishi.

> **Vaqt:** 20 daqiqa. **Band 8+ uchun:** Rasmiy xatda tabiiy bog‘lovchilar: I am writing to enquire about…, I would be grateful if…
